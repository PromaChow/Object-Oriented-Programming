A school wants to keep its records on a computer. The school has teachers and students. Both are people. A person has a name, an age and an address.

A student also has a student number and a grade level. A teacher also has a salary and a subject, such as math or English.

The school offers many courses. A course has a name, a code and a room number. One teacher teaches a course. But one teacher can teach many courses. A student can join many courses, and a course has many students.

Each student gets marks for each course. The mark has a score and a date. The system must work out the average score of a student. It must also work out the average score of a course.

The school has a principal. The principal is a teacher too. The principal can add a new course, remove a course and change the room of a course.

At the end of the year, the school prints a report card for each student. The report card shows the student’s name, all courses, all marks and the average. A report card belongs to one student only.

Sometimes a teacher is sick. Then another teacher takes the class for one day. The system should keep this information.
