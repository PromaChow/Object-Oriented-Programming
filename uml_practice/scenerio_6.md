A start-up wants to make a food delivery app. There are three types of users: customers, restaurant owners and delivery drivers. All users have a name, an email, a phone number and a password.

A restaurant owner can add a restaurant. A restaurant has a name, an address, opening hours and a menu. A menu has many items. An item has a name, a price, a photo and a list of ingredients. Some items are vegetarian. An owner can mark an item as “not available” if it is sold out.

A customer can search for restaurants by name, food type or rating. The customer picks items and adds them to an order. An order can contain items from one restaurant only. The customer can add a note, such as “no onions”.

At checkout, the customer enters a delivery address. The system works out the delivery fee from the distance. The customer can use a discount code. A code has a percent off and an end date. The customer then pays online by card or by digital wallet.

After payment, the restaurant gets a notification. The restaurant accepts the order and starts cooking. Then the system finds the nearest free driver. The driver gets a request and can accept or refuse it. If the driver refuses, the system asks the next driver.

The order has a status: “placed”, “cooking”, “on the way” and “delivered”. The customer can watch the driver’s position on a map in real time. Each time the status changes, the customer gets a message.

After delivery, the customer can rate the restaurant and the driver from one to five stars and write a comment. The app shows the average rating.

If an order is late or wrong, the customer can ask for a refund. A support worker checks the case and decides.
