A small pet shop wants a system to manage its animals. The shop sells dogs, cats and birds. Every pet has a name, an age, a color and a price. Every pet can also eat and sleep.

Dogs can bark and do tricks. Cats can meow and climb. Birds can fly and sing. A bird also has a wing size.

Each pet lives in a cage. A cage has a number and a size. One cage holds only one pet at a time.

The shop has workers. A worker has a name and a phone number. A worker feeds the pets and cleans the cages. The shop also has a manager. The manager is a worker too, but the manager can also set the price of a pet.

A customer can visit the shop and buy a pet. When a customer buys a pet, the shop makes a receipt. The receipt shows the date, the pet’s name and the total price. One receipt is for one pet only.

The shop owner wants to see a list of all pets that are not sold yet. The owner also wants to see all receipts for one day.
