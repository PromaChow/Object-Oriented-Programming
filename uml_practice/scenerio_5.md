A company wants to build an online shop. A visitor can look at products without an account. To buy something, the visitor must register and become a customer. A customer has a name, an email, a password and a delivery address.

The shop has many products. A product has a name, a price, a description and a stock number. Products belong to categories, such as “clothes” or “phones”. One category can have many products. A category can also have smaller sub-categories.

A customer can add products to a shopping cart. The cart shows each product, the quantity and the total price. The customer can change the quantity or remove a product.

When the customer is ready, the customer clicks “checkout”. The system then creates an order. An order has a number, a date, a status and a list of order items. The status can be “new”, “paid”, “sent” or “delivered”.

The customer must pay for the order. There are two ways to pay: by credit card or by cash on delivery. Credit card payment needs a card number and an expiry date. Cash payment needs no extra details.

After payment, the system lowers the stock of each product. If a product is out of stock, the system must tell the customer.

The shop also has an admin. The admin can add products, change prices and see all orders.

Customers can write a review for a product they bought. A review has a star rating and a comment
